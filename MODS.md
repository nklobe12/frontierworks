# Frontierworks mod list

114 mods, all written for this pack. Generated by tools/build.py.

## Materials (8)

| Mod | ID | What it adds |
|---|---|---|
| Metals | `fw_metals` | Eight new ores - tin, lead, silver, nickel, zinc, aluminum, platinum and uranium - with raw ores, ingots, nuggets, dusts and storage blocks, plus dusts for vanilla metals. |
| Alloys | `fw_alloys` | Bronze, brass, steel, electrum, invar, constantan and duralumin - the alloys every Frontierworks machine is built from. Mix dusts by hand early on, then use the Alloy Kiln or Alloy Smelter. |
| Parts | `fw_parts` | Plates, gears, rods and wires for every metal, hand crafting tools, and the machine frames, motors and coils that all Frontierworks machines are assembled from. |
| Gems | `fw_gems` | Ruby, sapphire, peridot, topaz and onyx: deep ores, gem blocks, and the gems used for jewellery, relics and high-tier tools. |
| Minerals | `fw_minerals` | Sulfur, saltpeter, graphite, fluorite and apatite ores - the raw chemistry behind explosives, fertilizer, batteries and reactor parts. |
| Crystals | `fw_crystals` | Crystal geodes deep underground: Energite powers advanced circuits and batteries, Voidite bends space for teleporters. Budding blocks regrow crystals over time. |
| Oil | `fw_oil` | Crude oil lakes in deserts, badlands and plains, plus oil sand deposits. Refine crude into diesel, lubricant and plastics, and pave roads with fast-walking asphalt. |
| Circuits | `fw_circuits` | Silicon, wafers and the four circuit tiers - basic, advanced, elite and quantum - plus the Circuit Assembler that builds them efficiently. |
