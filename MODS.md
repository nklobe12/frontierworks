# Lodestar mod list

18 mods, all written for this pack. Generated by tools/build.py.

## Materials (4)

| Mod | ID | What it adds |
|---|---|---|
| Deep Veins | `ls_veins` | Ten new metals that form huge ore veins winding through the rock, with outcrops on the surface above them. Strike rock with a prospector's pick to hear which ores lie nearby and in which direction, or power a seismic surveyor to chart every ore under a 5x5 chunk area layer by layer. |
| Metalwork | `ls_metalwork` | Work metal by hand: heat it in a smith's forge (bellows make it hotter), then shape it on the smithing anvil with hammer strikes while it glows. Hot metal burns bare hands, so carry it with tongs or quench it in a cauldron. Smelt steel in a fire-brick bloomery, mix bronze, brass, electrum and five more alloys in the alloy kiln, and make plates, rods, gears and wires. |
| Lapidary | `ls_lapidary` | Eight gems grow in geodes deep underground: garnet, topaz, peridot, sapphire, ruby, opal, onyx and aquamarine. Break full clusters for rough stones, cut them on a powered cutting wheel as cabochons, step cuts or brilliants (harder cuts give stronger gems but may shatter), then set them into sockets on your tools, weapons and armor at the jeweler's table for bonuses that scale with cut and quality. Gem blocks and gem lamps for building. |
| Petroleum | `ls_petroleum` | Oil lies in reservoirs under some chunks; a geophone tells you where, and oil seeps and tar pits on the surface give it away. Pump it with a pumpjack, split it into heavy oil, diesel, kerosene, naphtha and gas in a distillation column as tall as the fractions you want, crack and reform the heavy and light ends, and turn the gas into plastics and synthetic rubber. Bitumen makes asphalt roads you walk faster on. |

|---|---|---|

## Tools, Gear & Gadgets (1)

| Mod | ID | What it adds |
|---|---|---|
| Toolsmith | `ls_toolsmith` | Build tools from parts. Each part can be made of a different material, and every material brings its own stats and trait. Shape wood, stone, bone and gems into parts on the part builder, or melt metals in a foundry of scorched bricks and pour them over gold casts. Assemble pickaxes, axes, shovels, swords and kamas at the tool station; hammers, broad axes, excavators and scythes at the tool forge. Tools level up as you use them, gain modifier slots, and never break: they just stop working until repaired. |
