# Lodestar mod list

## Materials (2)

| Mod | ID | What it adds |
|---|---|---|
| Deep Veins | `ls_veins` | Ten new metals that form huge ore veins winding through the rock, with outcrops on the surface above them. Strike rock with a prospector's pick to hear which ores lie nearby and in which direction, or power a seismic surveyor to chart every ore under a 5x5 chunk area layer by layer. |
| Metalwork | `ls_metalwork` | Work metal by hand: heat it in a smith's forge (bellows make it hotter), then shape it on the smithing anvil with hammer strikes while it glows. Hot metal burns bare hands, so carry it with tongs or quench it in a cauldron. Smelt steel in a fire-brick bloomery, mix bronze, brass, electrum and five more alloys in the alloy kiln, and make plates, rods, gears and wires. |

| Mod | ID | What it adds |
|---|---|---|
| Toolsmith | `ls_toolsmith` | Build tools from parts. Each part can be made of a different material, and every material brings its own stats and trait. Shape wood, stone, bone and gems into parts on the part builder, or melt metals in a foundry of scorched bricks and pour them over gold casts.
